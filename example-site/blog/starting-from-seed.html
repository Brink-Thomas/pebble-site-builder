Starting from seed

This spring I tried growing everything from seed on the kitchen windowsill. Some things worked beautifully, others… not so much.

What worked

  • Tomatoes: every single seed came up
  • Basil, once it was warm enough

What didn't

Courgettes hate being moved. Next year they go straight into the ground.
