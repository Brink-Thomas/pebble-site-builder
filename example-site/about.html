About me

I'm a mother of three who finally has time for the garden. For years I only had a few pots on the balcony; now I have a real patch of soil and far too many seed packets.

I live in a small village with clay soil, cold springs and a lot of slugs.

Why I started this blog

Mostly to remember what I planted where! But also because the best tips I ever got came from neighbours over the fence. This is my fence.
